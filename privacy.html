Introduction

Welcome to Aaryx AI.

Your privacy matters to us.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it when you visit aaryxai.com, use First Look, request a First-Fix Guide, or engage with our services.

Who We Are

Aaryx AI provides diagnostic, product, and implementation services for service businesses, including First Look, First-Fix Reviews, communication-risk tools, and workflow execution systems.

Our website allows visitors to receive an ungated First Look result and, if they choose, request a complimentary First-Fix Guide.

Information We Collect

First Look Result

First Look is ungated. Choosing an operating pattern produces an on-screen directional result without requiring your name or email.

First-Fix Guide Request

Only if you request a guide do we collect your first name, work email, selected operating pattern, and the corresponding guide requested.

Technical Information

For guide requests, we record limited operational metadata, including:

  • request and consent timestamps
  • source page path
  • delivery status and related delivery-attempt information
  • a non-reversible, IP-derived hash used for abuse prevention

First Look Guide Requests

When you request a free AARYX First-Fix Guide, we collect your first name, work email, selected operating pattern, corresponding guide, request and consent timestamps, source page path, delivery status, and your marketing-consent choice. We also create a limited, non-reversible, IP-derived hash to help prevent automated abuse.

We use this information to deliver the exact guide requested, maintain a record of the request, troubleshoot delivery, and understand which guides are useful. Requesting a guide does not subscribe you to promotional communications. We send marketing communications only when you separately opt in, and you may withdraw that consent at any time.

How We Use Information

We use information we collect to:

  • provide First Look and deliver requested First-Fix Guides
  • respond to enquiries
  • schedule consultations
  • improve services
  • communicate product updates where you have opted in
  • improve website performance

We never sell personal information.

Marketing

We send occasional educational or promotional updates only where you have separately opted in. You may unsubscribe from those communications at any time. Transactional messages needed to deliver a guide you requested are not promotional updates.

Third Party Services

We may use third party services in connection with the website and our operations, including:

  • Vercel
  • Supabase (secure data storage and private guide-file storage)
  • Resend (transactional delivery of requested guides)
  • Google Tag Manager
  • Google Analytics 4

Applicable analytics services may include Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 4, which help us understand site usage and conversion activity. Each provider maintains its own privacy policy.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and operational safeguards designed to protect information we collect and store.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we do not promise absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, respond to enquiries, maintain business records, and satisfy legal or operational requirements.

Our operating retention schedule is subject to founder approval and may be updated as the service evolves.

Your Rights

You may request:

  • access
  • correction
  • deletion
  • marketing opt-out

To make a request, contact aaryxaiteam@gmail.com. We may request reasonable information to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Preserved Legacy Blueprint Infrastructure

Earlier Blueprint infrastructure and records are preserved for operational continuity and are not the current public First Look or First-Fix Guide flow. This policy describes the active public flow above.

Children

Our services are intended for business owners and professionals. They are not intended for individuals under 18.

Direct Answers

Common Pittsburgh buyer questions

What does a workflow automation company actually do?

A workflow automation company maps how work moves through the business, finds the choke points, and builds the triggers, handoffs, and visibility layers that keep the process moving.

How is workflow automation different from AI automation?

Workflow automation focuses on the process chain and handoffs. AI automation adds intelligence where decisions, classification, drafting, or risk detection improve that workflow.

What problems does workflow automation solve for Pittsburgh businesses?

It helps with missed handoffs, slow approvals, repeated data entry, unclear ownership, status-chasing, and client friction caused by internal process gaps.

Should I start with workflow automation or AI consulting?

Start with workflow automation when the bottleneck is already visible. Start with consulting when the business knows something is broken but cannot yet see where the real operational drag begins.

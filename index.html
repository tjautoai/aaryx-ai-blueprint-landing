FAQ

The practical questions.

Should I start with Trackt or Blueprint?

Start with Trackt if your sharpest problem is missed follow-up, buried commitments, unanswered questions, and not knowing what needs action now. Start with Blueprint if you know something operational is breaking but the real issue is still unclear.

What if my problem is bigger than Trackt?

That is exactly why AARYX Implementation exists. The Blueprint will tell you whether the issue needs a broader workflow system rather than a communication-risk product first.

Is AARYX selling generic AI consulting?

No. AARYX sells contained systems and clearer operational outcomes. Technology is the mechanism, not the product people are being asked to decode.

Do you work only with Pittsburgh businesses?

No. Pittsburgh pages remain for local search intent. The core AARYX offer is built for service businesses beyond one city.

What if I already have a CRM?

That does not remove the need for better follow-through visibility or stronger workflow execution. AARYX is meant to solve what current tools are still missing.
